# Fundraising Landscape: A CTO's Guide

This document summarizes the evolving responsibilities and focus areas for a CTO during different fundraising stages.

## 1. Fundraising Stages & CTO Responsibilities

| Stage | Core Focus | CTO Coding % | Key Challenge | Fundraising Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seed** | Product-Market Fit (PMF) | 70-90% | Speed to market | Visionary / Builder |
| **Series A** | Scaling the Product | 20-40% | Technical debt / Stability | Technical Auditor |
| **Series B** | Scaling the Organization | < 10% | People / Process | Strategic Executive |

## 2. Key Fundraising Metrics VCs Care About

*   **Seed:** Team quality, vision, initial traction (user growth, engagement), technical "moat".
*   **Series A:** PMF proof (revenue, churn, CAC/LTV), scalability of architecture, team depth.
*   **Series B:** Operational efficiency, unit economics (contribution margin, payback period), technical roadmap aligning with business goals.

## 3. Demonstrating Fundraising Experience in Interviews

*   **Focus on Outcomes:** Instead of listing technologies, explain *how* your technical decisions accelerated the business at different stages.
*   **Due Diligence Ready:** Highlight experience in preparing for technical due diligence (e.g., maintaining clean code, clear architecture diagrams, robust security policies).
*   **Strategic Vision:** Show how you bridged the gap between technical limitations and commercial goals to secure funding.

## 4. Recommended Resources

*   *VC Pitch Deck Teardowns* (e.g., Y Combinator videos) - to understand the investor mindset.
*   *Engineering Management Books* (e.g., "The Manager's Path") - for transition from coder to leader.
*   *Startup Financial Modeling* - to understand how technical costs (servers, hiring) affect the company's runway.
